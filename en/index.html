About me

Making model outputs
useful to people.

I study Software Science at Dankook University and work as an undergraduate researcher in the AIV Lab, focusing on computer vision and 3D pose estimation. I’m interested in turning human movement in video into measurements and descriptions, then checking how well they reflect the action.

In soccer dribbling analysis, I designed and reviewed metrics based on body landmarks and ball motion. In my research on data for robot learning, I compare how action segments, person identities, and 3D meshes can be linked consistently.

At a medical AI hackathon, I worked on MCP retrieval and response generation. With Danzzan, I tackled ticketing and operational problems at university festivals. Working on both model experiments and user-facing services has taught me to look at problems through data and system behavior together.

When results differ from expectations, I return to the source footage and logs. I document failures alongside successes so the next experiment has a clear basis. I want to keep studying visual understanding and build AI systems that people can put to use.
