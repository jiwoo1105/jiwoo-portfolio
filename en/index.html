A little about Jiwoo.

I build with an understanding
of how things work.

I’m Jiwoo Park, an undergraduate researcher in the AIV Lab at Dankook University, working on computer vision and 3D motion analysis. I’m interested in turning movement in video into meaningful data and building AI features people can use.

As I build, I ask why the code behaves as it does and why a design fits the problem. When results differ from what I expect, I examine the input data and each processing stage, using experiments and logs to investigate the cause.

Through computer vision and medical AI projects, as well as a festival service, I’ve learned to examine the evidence behind research results and consider the situations users encounter. I aim to be an AI engineer who can explain technical choices and work with a team to find better solutions.
